What do we do?

EA Heidelberg is a university group and social hub for people who are interested in making the world a better place in an evidence-guided way. We regularly host events that explore the most pressing global problems, exciting new ways to solve them, and how we concretely can make a big difference.

What is EA?

Effective Altruism (EA) is a global movement that combines a research field identifying the most pressing global problems and their solutions with a community of people acting on these findings through their careers, their donations, or their volunteering.
