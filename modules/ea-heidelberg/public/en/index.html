Effective Altruism Heidelberg

Good intentions don't save anyone. Good evidence does.

We're a student group in Heidelberg that spends evenings figuring out which ways of helping people, animals, and the future actually work best — then meets the people doing them. No background in philosophy, economics, or anything else required. Just curiosity and an evening to spare.

Why this matters

Not all ways of helping are equal

Some charities and interventions do ten, a hundred, sometimes a thousand times more good per euro than others working on the same problem. That's not a reason to give up on helping — it's the single best argument for thinking harder about where your time, attention, and money go.

In three sentences

What is effective altruism?

🔍

Use evidence, not just instinct

Figure out what actually helps, using research and data — not just what feels generous.

⚖️

Prioritise ruthlessly

Some problems are bigger, more neglected, and more solvable than others. Work on those first.

🚀

Then actually act

Through your career, your donations, or your time — ideas only matter once you put them into practice.
